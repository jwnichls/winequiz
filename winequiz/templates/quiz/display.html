Welcome to the 4th wine competition!

This time we will experiment with using our phones to enter our answers as we blind taste the wines. Here's what to do to get set up:


1. Scan the QR code to the left with your phone

2. Tap the "Join" button next to "4th Wine Competition"

3. Enter a unique name for yourself

4. Tap the "Join" button

5. Wait for the competition to begin


If you lose the web page, just scan the QR code again. You should not need to enter your name again.

All wines that we are tasting today are testable for the Advanced Sommelier exam. Copies of the list of testable wines can be found around the space.
